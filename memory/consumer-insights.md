### 2. Culture Productization Framework ⭐ Priority 2
**Status:** Finalized framework, integrated into Agentic Film Framework
**Objective:** Convert/adapt any story to a different culture at screenplay level

**The "Culture Architecture" Framework:**
- Decodes culture through fundamental elements (ideology + material world interaction)
- Examines visible AND invisible markers:
  - **Intentional cognitive acts** (conscious choices)
  - **Phenomenology** (unconscious bodily movements): embodiment, habitus, affect

**Current implementation:**
- Injected into agentic film framework's adaptation tool
- Can change detailed screenplay based on culture architecture
- Output = instructional manual/taxonomy to help agent make sense of information

**Primary challenge:** Information for cultural adaptation sourced mostly from limited internet pool

**HMT suggestion:** Proactively build a repository of cultural nuances directly into the agent — create a body of knowledge about various regions

**Quality observation:** Current AI films (despite cultural adaptations) look similar — yellow/dusty gradients, similar facial features, timing. HMT suggested AI-generated films may evolve into a **specific content category** rather than mainstream consumption.
