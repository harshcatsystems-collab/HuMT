## Key Narrative Arc

### 1. Strategic Pivot (15 months)
- Paused performance marketing intentionally
- Shifted from growth-driven acquisition → retention-first
- Three tactical levers deployed:
  1. **Lifecycle messaging** (CLM)
  2. **Player/product improvements**
  3. **Content strategy shifts**

**Result:** M0 moved from ~13% → peak near 45%

### 2. Content Strategy Wins
| Format | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **Long series** (50+ episodes, weekly) | 90% week-to-week retention |
| **Microdramas** | Integrated on main platform (not standalone) — higher retention + discovery |
| **Web series** | Lowest cancellation rates — shifting acquisition emphasis here |

### 3. Market Profitability
| Market | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Haryanvi | ✅ Cash-flow profitable |
| Rajasthani | ✅ Cash-flow profitable |
| Bhojpuri | 2-3 months to profitability (~$5.1–5.9M ARR, started Jun-Jul) |

### 4. AI Transformation (The Big Bet)

**Philosophy:** "Business must be rebuilt from ground up for AI, not incrementally adapted"

**Agentic Pipeline:**
- 70 specialized production agents
- 11-agent cultural adaptation system
- 16 categories, 155 cultural markers in taxonomy

**Capabilities:**
- Generate casting, costumes, locations, storyboards, video from script OR existing movie
- License actor likenesses by age/face rights (no actor time required)
- Master frame → camera-specific shots (preserves directorial "taste")
- Source-to-output: <1 hour with 1 person

**Demo shown:** Korean cultural adaptation of Haryanvi film (fully workflow-generated)

**Timeline:** ~2 more weeks to production-grade results

### 5. Concierge AI
- Personalizes homepage content (2x CTR on title changes)
- Customer support at scale: 90% CSAT, 30% refund reduction
- Self-hosted enterprise intelligence (open-source models, fine-tuned on internal data)

### 6. Character Agents
- High user engagement
- Expanding to audio/video interactions
- Will inform sequels and episodic plots

### 7. Entertainment Extensions
- Games + Cricket second-screen experience
- Retention drivers + potential top-of-funnel
