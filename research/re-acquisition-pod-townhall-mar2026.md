## Three Operations

### Operation Hunt — Paid Performance Marketing

| # | Tactic | Details |
|---|--------|---------|
| 01 | **Content Re-targeting** | Identified what each user watched. Re-targeted with sequels & related content. Example: Watched Punarjanam? → Showed Maha Punarjanam. |
| 02 | **Scaled on Right Channels** | Deployed Meta App Campaigns, Meta Web Campaigns, and Google App Campaigns. Continuously A/B tested creatives and optimized by content performance. |
| 03 | **The Result** | Gold cohort SR: **30%** vs. general cohort: **10%**. High-relevance creative = high CTR. **3x higher subscription rate** from same ad spend. |

### Operation Decoy — Pricing Psychology

**Multi-Tiered Subscription Display:**

| Plan | Price | Framing |
|------|-------|---------|
| Monthly | ₹199/month | Valid for 1 month |
| Quarterly (DECOY) | ₹166/month | SAVE 25%, Valid for 3 months |
| Yearly | ₹58/month | SAVE 70%, Valid for 1 year |

**How it works:**
> "The quarterly plan is the decoy. It makes ₹58/month feel like a no-brainer. Users compare quarterly to monthly, then see yearly as the obvious winner."

**Result:** 
- **2x Revenue Per User** vs. standard single-plan paywall
- First cycle data — LTV tracking ongoing
