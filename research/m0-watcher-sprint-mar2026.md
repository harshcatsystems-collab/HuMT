## Executive Summary

2-week sprint focused on driving M0 Week 1 Watcher % from **30% baseline to 36% target**. Week 1 is identified as the "make-or-break window" — 70% of all M0 watchers watch in Week 1. If they don't watch in Week 1, only 30% chance they'll watch at all during M0.

---

## North Star Metric

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **M0 Week 1 Watcher %** | % of new subscribers who watch at least one piece of content in Week 1 of Month 0 |
| **Baseline** | 30% |
| **Target** | 36% |
| **Current (Mar 18)** | 32% |

### Strategic Chain
1. Drive Week 1 Watcher % →
2. Lifts M0 Watcher % →
3. Improves Retention & LTV

## Key Learnings

1. **Direct subs > Web acquisition** — 35% of web acquisition subs had no watch activity during trial
2. **2-week plan scaling down** — Not driving quality engagement
3. **Google perf ads work** — Contributing 50% of inorganic traffic, 20% of daily conversions
4. **Tambola drives engagement** — Sunday players have 5-20pp higher watch rates
5. **Personalisation has signal** — +10% conversion, +22% CTR but still in testing
6. **AI calling doesn't lift** — No incremental effect vs control group
