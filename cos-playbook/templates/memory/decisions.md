# Decision Log

> Record significant decisions with context and rationale.
> Future-you will thank present-you.

---

## Why Log Decisions?

1. **Memory** — decisions fade; this file doesn't
2. **Accountability** — know who decided what and why
3. **Learning** — review past decisions to improve future ones
4. **Handoff** — anyone can understand the history
